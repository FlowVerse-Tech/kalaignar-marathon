## 2. In-Depth Analysis of Unassigned Photo Batches

### A. Folder `07.08.2024` — Royapettah Hospital Cancer Centre Milestone (12 Photos)
- **Visual Evidence:** Stage backdrop prominently reads *"Government Royapettah Hospital — Foundation Stone Laying Ceremony for New Cancer Treatment Building constructed with funds raised from Kalaignar Memorial International Marathon 2023"*. Dressed dignitaries, doctors, and engineers unveiling the project plaque.
- **Why Not Assigned to Edition Cards:** These photos document a major healthcare infrastructure milestone funded by the 2023 marathon proceeds, occurring on August 7, 2024. They do not depict race-day athletic events, so placing them in the 4 edition race cards would confuse participants looking for marathon race action.
- **Recommended Usage:**
  - Perfect for a future **"Healthcare Impact & Philanthropy"** case study or dedicated sub-page showing where the ₹3,00,00,000 proceeds went.
  - Can be added to an interactive "Hospital Projects" modal linked from the "Charity" purpose card.

### B. Folder `25.02.2026` — Institute of Child Health (ICH) Egmore Shelter (9 Photos)
- **Visual Evidence:** Ribbon cutting, bronze inaugural plaque, and interior photos of the newly constructed patient attendants rest house at Egmore Children's Hospital. Plaque reads: *"Built from the charitable proceeds of ₹1.22 Crore contributed by the Kalaignar Memorial Marathon 2022"*.
- **Folder Date Note:** The folder is labeled `25.02.2026` (or February 25, 2024/2026). Regardless of the exact archival naming, the visual content proves it is the physical delivery of the 2022 marathon's hospital pledge.
- **Why Not Assigned to Edition Cards:** Like the 2024 set, this is a post-race civic completion event rather than an athletic edition.
- **Recommended Usage:**
  - High-value proof of transparency. Best utilized in an **Annual Social Audit Report** or **Impact Gallery** showing the completed facility serving rural families.

### C. Folder `29.3.2022` — Burst Sequences & Duplicate Stage Angles (98 Photos)
- **Visual Evidence:** EXIF data, banners, and digital bibs prove these were captured on **August 7, 2022** (3rd Edition / first in-person event), despite the folder name `29.3.2022`.
- **Breakdown of Unassigned Shots:**
  - *Start line countdown camera bursts (42 photos):* Fast continuous bursts (e.g. `0R8A2050` through `0R8A2095` and `PVC_8250` through `PVC_8310`). We selected `PVC_8361.JPG` (the best single frame with maximum confetti and balloon spread) and `0R8A2085.JPG` (best flag-off frame).
  - *Stage felicitation and trophy handovers (31 photos):* Repeat podium angles of various age-group winners and medal presentations (`IMG00590` to `IMG00630`). We selected `IMG00596.JPG` (the clear memorial bust tribute) and `PVC_8620.JPG` (the ₹1.20 crore cheque handover).
  - *Course runner snaps (25 photos):* General photos of runners passing water tables and marshals. We selected `PVC_8195.JPG` and `PVC_8380.JPG` as the most vibrant, expressive representations.

### D. Folder `2023` — Aerial Drone Sequences & Adjudicator Bursts (20 Photos)
- **Breakdown of Unassigned Shots:**
